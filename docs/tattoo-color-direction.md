# Tattoo color and motif direction

Visual estimates, not color-calibrated samples:
- Heavy charcoal-black outlines: #252C2D
- Faded petrol blue / turquoise fill: #438C99
- Muted blue-gray shading: #647C7B
- Small warm yellow highlights: #C2A451
- Very small faded red accents: #A84C48
- Unfilled negative space; avoid multicolor bands across every shape.

Style: small separate American-traditional-influenced patchwork flash, bold contour lines, compact silhouettes, black shading, limited flat color. Motif families: flying saucer and four-point sparkles; electric guitar; eight-ball; insect; barbed-wire linework. Use original interpretations, not traced copies.

Only palette and tattoo-style notes retained. Source photographs are not website assets.

# PatchworkMD

The language of the PatchworkMD portfolio: the software it presents and the path from visitor to intro call.

## Products

**Product**:
A piece of software PatchworkMD presents on the site, such as Orbit, 2DO, or LocalModelMatch.
_Avoid_: Project (except in the homepage heading "Projects"), app, item

**Product page**:
The page at `/<slug>` that describes one Product. Orbit and OpenNGP have their own hand-built Product pages.
_Avoid_: Campaign page, landing page

**Stage**:
The coarse phase of a Product shown in the homepage index: Concept, In development, Release candidate, Public preview, Experimental alpha.
_Avoid_: Signal, status (when the coarse phase is meant)

**Release status**:
The exact, current way a visitor can or cannot get a Product, including build or version, such as "iOS beta · build 2 approved".
_Avoid_: Availability label, signal

**Alias**:
A retired slug that permanently redirects to a Product page, such as `localmodelfit`.
_Avoid_: Old URL, legacy route

**PatchworkMD Ops**:
The admin-automation service for small businesses, presented at `/ops`. It is a service, not a Product.
_Avoid_: agency, retainer

**Pilot**:
The first, fixed-price PatchworkMD Ops engagement, scoped in writing before work starts. Booked with a deposit; the rest is due on delivery.
_Avoid_: trial, sprint

**Care**:
The optional monthly PatchworkMD Ops service after a Pilot.
_Avoid_: retainer, maintenance plan

## Spelling

**2DO**:
The task app for Mac and iPhone. Its Product page slug is `2d0`. `/2do` is its landing page, with Marky (the app's marker mascot) and one Join the TestFlight beta button; `/2do/privacy` and `/2do/support` are its App Store pages. Prices, the support email, and `TESTFLIGHT_URL` live in `app/2do/facts.ts`.
_Avoid_: 2D0, 2do, ToDo

**LocalModelMatch**:
The runtime-aware model-fit web app.
_Avoid_: LocalModelFit

**PatchworkMD**:
The studio and its GitHub organisation. The wordmark is written patchwork.md.
_Avoid_: Patchwork, PWMD

## Conversations

**Intro call**:
A first conversation a visitor books, or requests by email, to talk through a problem and whether it fits.
_Avoid_: Consultation, discovery call, sales call

**Lead**:
A person who has asked for an Intro call.
_Avoid_: Prospect, customer, contact

**Referrer**:
Someone who already knows Austin's work and suggests a person who might need it.
_Avoid_: Affiliate, partner

**Introduction**:
A person a Referrer has suggested who has not yet asked for an Intro call. They are contacted only after giving permission. An Introduction becomes a Lead when they ask for one.
_Avoid_: Prospect, contact

**Referral**:
The private record of one Introduction from one Referrer, from receipt to outcome.
_Avoid_: Affiliate signup, partner lead

**Referral stage**:
Where a Referral sits in its lifecycle: received, permission confirmed, introduced, call booked, qualified, proposal, won or closed.
_Avoid_: Stage (that is a Product's coarse phase), status

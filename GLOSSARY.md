# PatchworkMD

The language of the PatchworkMD portfolio: the software it presents and the path from visitor to intro call.

## Products

**Product**:
A piece of software PatchworkMD presents on the site, such as Orbit, 2DO, or LocalModelMatch.
_Avoid_: Project (except in the homepage heading "Projects"), app, item

**Product page**:
The page at `/<slug>` that describes one Product. Orbit and OpenNGP have their own hand-built Product pages.
_Avoid_: Campaign page, landing page

**Stage**:
The coarse phase of a Product shown in the homepage index: Concept, In development, Release candidate, Public preview, Experimental alpha.
_Avoid_: Signal, status (when the coarse phase is meant)

**Release status**:
The exact, current way a visitor can or cannot get a Product, including build or version, such as "iOS beta · build 2 approved".
_Avoid_: Availability label, signal

**Alias**:
A retired slug that permanently redirects to a Product page, such as `localmodelfit`.
_Avoid_: Old URL, legacy route

## Spelling

**2DO**:
The task app for Mac and iPhone. Its Product page slug is `2d0`.
_Avoid_: 2D0, 2do, ToDo

**LocalModelMatch**:
The runtime-aware model-fit web app.
_Avoid_: LocalModelFit

**PatchworkMD**:
The studio and its GitHub organisation. The wordmark is written patchwork.md.
_Avoid_: Patchwork, PWMD

## Conversations

**Intro call**:
A first conversation a visitor books, or requests by email, to talk through a problem and whether it fits.
_Avoid_: Consultation, discovery call, sales call

**Lead**:
A person who has asked for an Intro call.
_Avoid_: Prospect, customer, contact

**Referral**:
A Lead introduced by someone who already knows Austin's work, with the Lead's consent.
_Avoid_: Affiliate, partner signup
